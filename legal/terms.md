## 1. What Slab Guard does
Slab Guard analyzes photos and videos you provide of graded and slabbed collectibles and produces a **risk report**: what could be verified (for example, against a grader's public certification records), what looks inconsistent, what could not be assessed from your media, and what to check next.

## 2. Reports are guidance, not a guarantee
**Slab Guard never certifies that an item is authentic or counterfeit.** Reports are based only on the media and records available, use automated analysis that can be wrong, and can miss counterfeits or flag genuine items. Do not rely on a report as your sole basis for buying, selling, insuring or grading decisions. For high-value items, confirm with the grading company or a qualified expert.
