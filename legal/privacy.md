## What we collect
See the **Notice at Collection** for the full list. In short: account details, the photos/videos you upload (with location and device metadata **removed on upload**), certification numbers and grader records, reports, subscription status, and service usage data.

## How we use it
To provide reports and your history; to process subscriptions; to keep the service secure (fraud and abuse prevention, rate limiting); to measure service quality and costs; and — **only with your separate opt-in** — to improve our detection models.

## What we do not do
We do **not sell** your personal information and do **not share** it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use your uploads for model training without your opt-in.

## Who processes data for us
Hosting/database/storage and authentication (e.g. Supabase); payments (Stripe; Apple and Google for in-app purchases, via RevenueCat); optional reverse-image search (Google Cloud Vision — receives your front photo when enabled for your plan); optional AI-written explanations (Anthropic — receives the report's text findings, never your photos or account details); grader certification lookups (receive only the certification number). Original photos/videos: deleted automatically **12 months** after upload (configurable). Derived analysis images follow the same schedule. Reports and history: kept until you delete them or your account. Consent records and billing records: kept as required by law. [Counsel: confirm periods.]

## Your rights (including California residents)
Access (download your data in Account → Privacy), deletion (Account → Delete account), correction, opting out of model training at any time, and non-discrimination for exercising rights. ## Security
Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, private storage, least-privilege staff access with two-factor authentication, audit logs. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and authorities of breaches as required by law.
