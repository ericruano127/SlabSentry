> **DRAFT — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.**

You can choose whether the photos and videos you upload may be used to **improve Slab Guard's detection** (for example, to measure accuracy and to train or tune models that spot counterfeit slabs).

- **Optional.** Saying no does not limit any feature.
- **Scope.** Only uploads made while this consent is on. Location and device metadata are already removed. Training copies are linked to this consent record.
- **Change anytime** in Account → Privacy. If you opt out, your uploads are excluded from future training and evaluation sets. [Counsel/engineering: define handling of models already trained.]
- **Deletion.** Deleting your account removes your uploads from training sets.
